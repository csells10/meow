## Level 4 Rule Draft

A claim may receive firmer language only when:

- `two_way_context = supportive`
- metric is allowlisted
- claim_type / claim_layer is allowlisted
- sample_size meets minimum threshold
- validation_rate meets threshold
- metric is not blocked or unstable
