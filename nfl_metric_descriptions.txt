🔥 SCORING & EFFICIENCY

actual_points
  Formula: Total points scored by the team
  Meaning: Direct outcome of performance. Higher values indicate more scoring success.
  Higher is better

points_per_play
  Formula: points_scored / total_plays
  Meaning: Measures scoring efficiency per snap. Useful to compare high-tempo vs methodical offenses.
  Higher is better

points_allowed_per_play
  Formula: points_allowed / opponent_total_plays
  Meaning: Defensive efficiency per snap. Reflects how much damage is done against the defense on each play.
  Lower is better

td_rate
  Formula: (passing_tds + rushing_tds) / total_plays
  Meaning: Touchdown scoring frequency. A measure of finishing drives effectively.
  Higher is better

🟥 RED ZONE & CONVERSION

red_zone_efficiency
  Formula: red_zone_tds / red_zone_attempts
  Meaning: Effectiveness at turning red zone visits into touchdowns.
  Higher is better

third_down_pct
  Formula: third_down_conversions / third_down_attempts
  Meaning: Ability to extend drives. Indicates offensive rhythm and clutch performance.
  Higher is better

fourth_down_pct
  Formula: fourth_down_conversions / fourth_down_attempts
  Meaning: Measures aggressiveness and situational efficiency. Useful in analyzing coaching decisions.
  Higher is better

🔁 PLAY CALLING & STRATEGY

run_play_pct
  Formula: rushing_attempts / total_plays
  Meaning: Share of plays that are runs. Indicates offensive approach — ball control or clock management.
  Context dependent

pass_play_pct
  Formula: pass_attempts / total_plays
  Meaning: Share of plays that are passes. Often reflects quarterback trust and game script.
  Context dependent

pass_run_ratio
  Formula: pass_attempts / rushing_attempts
  Meaning: Indicates balance or skew in play-calling. A ratio near 1.0 is balanced; higher favors the pass.
  Context dependent

🔄 SUSTAINED DRIVES & MOVEMENT

1st_down_rate
  Formula: first_downs / total_plays
  Meaning: Frequency of moving the chains. Reflects offensive consistency.
  Higher is better

pass_td_share
  Formula: passing_tds / (passing_tds + rushing_tds)
  Meaning: TD split toward passing. Highlights team identity or QB performance.
  Context dependent

rush_td_share
  Formula: rushing_tds / (passing_tds + rushing_tds)
  Meaning: TD split toward the run. Suggests red zone ground game strength.
  Context dependent

🧮 SNAP LOAD & USAGE

offensive_snap_load
  Formula: total_offensive_snaps / total_snaps
  Meaning: Share of total plays spent on offense. Indicates ability to control possession and tempo.
  Higher is better

defensive_snap_load
  Formula: total_defensive_snaps / total_snaps
  Meaning: Share of time spent on defense. A high value may suggest offensive inefficiency or poor time of possession.
  Lower is better

special_teams_snap_pct
  Formula: total_special_teams_snaps / total_snaps
  Meaning: Indicates special teams’ presence. Useful for spotting game script extremes or field position battles.
  Context dependent

💥 PRESSURE & TURNOVERS

sack_to_turnover_ratio
  Formula: sacks / (defensive_interceptions + ε)
  Meaning: Balance of pressure vs ball-hawking.
    A lower ratio implies more turnovers per sack (opportunistic defense),
    while a higher ratio reflects pressure-heavy but possibly inefficient defenses.
  Context dependent

pressure_rate
  Formula: (sacks + sacks_taken) / pass_attempts
  Meaning: Measures how often pressure occurs on passing downs — either applied or absorbed.
    Can reflect OL weakness or defensive disruption.
  Lower is better (for offense) / Higher is better (for defense)

turnover_margin
  Formula: (defensive_interceptions + fumbles_recovered) - (interceptions_thrown + fumbles_lost)
  Meaning: Net possession gain via turnovers. Key driver of game outcome.
  Higher is better

🛡 DEFENSIVE CONTROL

defensive_success_rate
  Formula: yards_allowed / opponent_total_plays
  Meaning: Average yards allowed per play. Lower means fewer big gains — indicates stingy defense.
  Lower is better

points_allowed_per_yard
  Formula: points_allowed / yards_allowed
  Meaning: Points yielded per yard given up. High value suggests red zone struggles or bend-and-break defense.
  Lower is better

📈 RAW OUTPUT

passing_yards
  Formula: Total passing yards gained
  Meaning: Measures aerial productivity.
  Higher is better

rushing_yards
  Formula: Total rushing yards gained
  Meaning: Reflects ground game strength.
  Higher is better

total_yards
  Formula: Passing + Rushing yards;
  Meaning: Overall offensive yardage production.
  Higher is better

sacks
  Formula: Total defensive sacks
  Meaning: Measures pass rush productivity and pressure.
  Higher is better

defensive_interceptions
  Formula: Total interceptions made by the defense
  Meaning: Indicator of playmaking and ball-hawking secondary.
  Higher is better

time_of_possession
  Formula: Time with ball in minutes (from MM:SS)
  Meaning: Field control and offensive endurance.
  Higher is generally better
